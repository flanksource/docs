Mission Control allows nothing by default: a new user can't see or do anything until a RoleBinding grants them access. So on a fresh installation, someone has to be able to sign in and create the first RoleBindings.

Mission Control solves this by shipping an **`admins` team**, already bound to the shipped `admin` and `plugin-admin` Roles. Whoever is in that team is an admin from their first sign-in. How the first people get into the team depends on how users sign in.

| Authentication | First admin                                                                  |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kratos         | The local `admin@local` user, which Mission Control creates in `admins`      |
| Basic auth     | The local `admin@local` user, which Mission Control creates in `admins`      |
| Clerk          | Your Clerk organization admins, who join `admins` when they sign in          |

## Kratos and basic auth

Mission Control creates a local user, `admin@local`, and adds it to the `admins` team every time it starts. Sign in as `admin@local` to set up access for everyone else.

<AdminPassword/>

The password comes from the `ADMIN_PASSWORD` environment variable, which the Helm chart sets from the `mission-control-admin-password` secret. The chart generates a random password when it creates the secret.

Once you're signed in:

1. Add the people who should be admins to the `admins` team from **Settings → Teams**.
2. Bind Roles to everyone else. For example, bind `mission-control-builtin/viewer` to `people: ["*"]` so every user can browse the catalog, then grant narrower Roles to the teams that need more. See the [Quickstart](/docs/guide/permissions/quickstart).

If you map your identity provider's groups to teams with a [team mapper](/docs/guide/permissions/team-mapping), you can return `admins` for your admin group instead of adding people by hand. A team mapper never removes `admin@local` from the team.

## Clerk

There's no `admin@local` with Clerk. Instead, your Clerk organization decides who the first admins are.

When a user signs in, Mission Control runs a team mapper over their Clerk session claims. Unless you configure your own with `--clerk-team-mapper`, the default mapper puts:

| Clerk user                                                     | Team      |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| Organization admins (`org_role` is `org:admin`, or `role` is `admin`) | `admins`  |
| Guests (`org_role` is `org:guest`)                             | No team   |
| Everyone else                                                  | `viewers` |

So your Clerk organization admins are Mission Control admins as soon as they sign in, and a user who leaves the admin role in Clerk leaves the `admins` team on their next request.

`viewers` isn't shipped. Until you create a team named `viewers` and bind a Role to it, such as `mission-control-builtin/viewer`, regular members of your organization can sign in but can't see anything.

If you write your own `--clerk-team-mapper`, make sure it still returns `admins` for the people who should be admins, or no one can manage access. See [Team Mapping](/docs/guide/permissions/team-mapping).

## External identity providers

Users who sign in through an [external identity provider](/docs/guide/permissions/external-identity-providers) never become admins on their own. A provider can only put its users in `admins` if its `allowed` list includes that team, and the provider reports a warning when it does.

## Getting admin access back

The `admins` team and its bindings can't be changed or deleted through the UI, the API or Kubernetes, and Mission Control recreates them on every start. A deny RoleBinding never covers RoleBindings, so it can't lock admins out either.

If every admin is lost, for example because all members were removed from `admins`:

- **Kratos and basic auth:** restart Mission Control. It adds `admin@local` back to `admins`.
- **Clerk:** sign in as a Clerk organization admin. The team mapper adds you back to `admins` on your first request.
