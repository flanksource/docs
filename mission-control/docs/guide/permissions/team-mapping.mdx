A **team mapper** puts people into Mission Control teams automatically when they sign in, based on their groups or roles in your login provider. It's a [CEL](/docs/reference/scripting/cel) expression that returns team names. You then bind Roles to those teams with [RoleBindings](/docs/guide/permissions/role-bindings#teams). Your login provider decides who belongs to which team, and Mission Control decides what each team may do.

A team mapper never grants a Role.

| Login                                                                 | Setting                                                            | Input                                                   |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Kratos, including [single sign-on](/docs/installation/self-hosted/sso) | The `identityRoleMapper` [Helm values](/docs/reference/helm/mission-control#identity-mapper), or the `--identity-role-mapper` flag. The name predates teams: it now returns teams | `identity`, the person's Kratos identity: traits and metadata |
| Clerk (Mission Control SaaS)                                          | `--clerk-team-mapper` flag                                         | `claims`, the claims of the verified Clerk session token |
| [External identity provider](/docs/guide/permissions/external-identity-providers) | `spec.identity.teams.mapper`                           | `claims`, the claims of the verified token              |

The flags take the expression inline, or a file path prefixed with `file://`. On a Helm install, set the Helm values instead of passing a flag.

## Output

The expression returns a list of team names:

```javascript title="clerk-team-mapper"
claims.org_role == "org:admin" ? ["clerk-admins"] : ["clerk-members"]
```

Kratos and Clerk mappers may also return `{"teams": [...]}`. An external identity provider's mapper must return a list.

An object result with any field other than `teams`, such as the `role` field older Kratos scripts returned, is logged as an error and changes nothing. Map those people into a team and bind the Role to the team instead:

```javascript title="identity-role-mapper"
{
  "teams": "admins" in identity.traits.groups ? ["admins"] : ["viewers"]
}
```

## Behavior

- **When it runs.** On a person's first request after they sign in, and again whenever the identity or claims it reads change. An external identity provider's mapper runs on every request.
- **Kept in sync.** The person's mapped teams become exactly what the mapper returns. They join new teams and leave mapped teams the mapper no longer returns. Teams an admin added them to directly are never touched.
- **Existing teams only.** A team that doesn't exist is skipped and logged. Create the team from **Settings → Teams**, and bind a Role to it, before a mapper returns it.
- **Without a mapper**, signing in to Kratos adds the person to no team.
- **Invites.** When you invite someone from **Settings → Users**, you choose their teams. They join those teams when they accept the invite.

:::warning
A mapper that reads attributes people can change themselves, such as Kratos traits the settings flow lets them edit, lets them choose their own teams. Map from attributes only admins or your identity provider can set, such as Kratos `metadata_public` or the Clerk organization role. The examples on this page read `identity.traits.groups`, which is only safe when your SSO claims mapper sets it on sign-in and people can't edit it in their Kratos profile settings.
:::

## Clerk default

When `--clerk-team-mapper` isn't set, Clerk uses this mapper. It puts organization admins in `admins`, guests in no team, and everyone else in `viewers`:

```javascript title="default-clerk-team-mapper"
{
  "teams":
    (has(claims.role) && claims.role == "admin") ||
    (has(claims.org_role) && claims.org_role == "org:admin") ? ["admins"] :
    (has(claims.org_role) && claims.org_role == "org:guest") ? [] :
    ["viewers"]
}
```

`admins` is the shipped team bound to `admin`. `viewers` grants nothing until you create it and bind a Role to it, for example `mission-control-builtin/viewer`.
