Permissions in Mission Control decide what each person, team or automation can see and do. For example, the platform team can run remediation playbooks on staging configs, everyone can browse the catalog, and only a few people can change connections.

Nothing is allowed by default. A new user can't see anything until you grant them access, so everything someone can do is written down in a place you can review.

## How it fits together

You write a grant with three custom resources, each describing one part of it:

- A [**Scope**](/docs/guide/permissions/scopes) picks out a set of resources, such as "every config tagged `env=staging`" or "the playbooks in the `monitoring` namespace".
- A [**Role**](/docs/guide/permissions/roles) lists what's allowed on those Scopes, such as "read the staging configs" or "run the monitoring playbooks on them". Write a Role once and reuse it for as many people as you need.
- A [**RoleBinding**](/docs/guide/permissions/role-bindings) gives a Role to people, teams, or to playbooks and notifications that act on their own. A RoleBinding can also narrow the Role to fewer resources, or refuse it outright.

A Scope or a Role on its own grants nothing. Access only starts when a RoleBinding connects a Role to someone.

## Example

This manifest lets `john@doe.com` run, approve and cancel any playbook on configs tagged `cluster=aws` or `cluster=demo`:

```yaml title="john-playbook-run.yaml" file=<rootDir>/modules/mission-control/playbook/testdata/rbac/allow-playbook.yaml

```

- `all-playbooks` and `aws-and-demo-configs` are the Scopes: the playbooks John may use, and the configs he may run them on.
- `john-playbook-run` is the Role. Each rule pairs an action with the playbooks it acts on (`resource`) and the configs it acts against (`target`).
- The RoleBinding of the same name gives the Role to John.

Running a playbook on a config also needs `read` on that config, so John also needs a Role that lets him read those configs.

:::warning Permission and PermissionGroup are deprecated
`Permission` and `PermissionGroup` resources no longer grant anything. Recreate them as Scopes, Roles, and RoleBindings. See [Migrating from Permissions](/docs/guide/permissions/migrating).
:::
